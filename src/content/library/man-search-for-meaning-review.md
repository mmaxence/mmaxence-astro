## A reminder to keep going

This is a book I turn to for inspiration and perspective. It reminds me that I can often go further than I think, especially when discomfort or discouragement makes stopping feel easier. There is something deeply encouraging in the human capacity to keep searching for meaning, even when circumstances offer very little.

## Remembering what I have

It also brings me back to how fortunate I am today. Safety, freedom, people I love, and the possibility of choosing what comes next can become so familiar that I stop noticing them. This book makes me pause and appreciate that foundation.

That does not make everyday difficulties unreal. It helps me see them in proportion, and approach them with more gratitude and determination. What stays with me is simple: appreciate the place I am in, make something of the possibilities I have, and keep going when things feel hard.
